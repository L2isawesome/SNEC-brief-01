Shanghai & Shenzhen

City 01

Shanghai

25 million people · financial powerhouse

"Shanghai to China is what New York to the US."

01 The old meets the hyper-new. 1930s Art Deco banks sit across from towers with facial-recognition lobbies. The tension is the story.
02 China's AI & fintech capital. Ant Group, ByteDance labs, Tesla's Gigafactory Asia — all here. The Pudong skyline is literally a monument to tech investment.
03 The showroom of modern China. Government-backed smart city pilots: autonomous delivery bots, cashless everything, AI traffic systems. Engineered to impress the world.

City 02

Shenzhen

18 million people · hardware capital of Earth

"Shenzhe is China's Silicon Valley."

01 Where gadgets are born. Huawei, DJI, BYD, Tencent — all headquartered here. If it has a chip in it, there's a good chance Shenzhen touched it.
02 The world's fastest factory floor. Makers go to Huaqiangbei market — a 10-storey building of every electronic component ever made — and prototype a product in 48 hours.
03 A city with zero nostalgia. It didn't exist in 1980. No ancient temples, no old districts. The entire DNA is: build fast, iterate, scale. Young, restless, relentless.

Event Spotlight

SNEC Shanghai

World's largest solar energy exhibition · Shanghai New International Expo Centre

SNEC is the annual heartbeat of the global solar industry, drawing hundreds of thousands of professionals, engineers, and investors to Shanghai each year. From cutting-edge photovoltaic panels to grid-scale storage and hydrogen technology, the expo floor is a live snapshot of where clean energy is headed. For our production, it's a chance to see China's green tech ambitions up close — the products, the people, and the sheer scale of it all.

Featured Companies

01

LONGi

Filmed at SNEC Shanghai

LONGi is the world's largest solar technology company, specialising in high-efficiency monocrystalline silicon wafers and modules. Founded in Xi'an in 2000, it has consistently broken world records for solar cell efficiency and today supplies panels to major projects across six continents. At SNEC, LONGi showcases its latest Hi-MO series — a benchmark for what utility-scale solar looks like in 2025.

02

SAJ Electric

Filmed at SNEC Shanghai

SAJ Electric is one of China's leading inverter and energy storage manufacturers, headquartered in Guangzhou. Its products sit at the heart of residential and commercial solar systems, converting DC power into usable AC electricity as efficiently as possible. SAJ has a strong foothold in Europe and is rapidly expanding its all-in-one home energy solutions — combining solar, storage, and smart monitoring in a single system.

Website

03

Anker SOLIX

Filmed at Shenzhen HQ

Anker SOLIX is the energy division of Anker Innovations — the Shenzhen-born brand best known for consumer electronics charging. SOLIX takes that same engineering obsession into portable and home energy storage: balcony solar kits, home batteries, and power stations designed for everyday consumers. It's a telling example of how Shenzhen's hardware culture is pivoting toward the energy transition, making clean power accessible and beautifully designed.
